Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This site

These pages are static. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and have no forms or accounts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log requests (such as IP address and browser type) under GitHub's privacy statement.

The channel

Late Copper publishes on YouTube. Viewing, comments and subscriptions are handled by YouTube under Google's privacy policy. We see only the aggregate statistics YouTube shows every channel; we never receive viewers' personal data.

The publishing tool

Late Copper uses a private tool to upload videos to its own channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. Its sign-in token is stored on that computer and nowhere else. It reads and writes only this channel's videos and their settings, and it never accesses anyone else's data. Nothing it handles is shared with third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the About page of the Late Copper channel on YouTube.
